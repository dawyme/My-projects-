Our Services

Comprehensive HVAC and refrigeration solutions for your home or business

Air Conditioning Repair

When your AC stops working, you need fast, reliable service. Our certified technicians diagnose and repair all major brands of air conditioning systems quickly and efficiently.

  • Diagnostic service
  • Refrigerant leak detection and repair
  • Compressor and condenser repairs
  • Electrical system troubleshooting
  • Thermostat repair and replacement
  • Airflow and ductwork issues
  • Same-day emergency service available

We service all types of systems including central air, ductless mini-splits, window units, and packaged systems.

Air Conditioning Installation

AC Installation Image

Proper installation is crucial for the efficiency and longevity of your air conditioning system. We provide professional installation services that ensure optimal performance and energy efficiency.

  • Free in-home consultation and load calculation
  • Proper system sizing for maximum efficiency
  • Professional installation following manufacturer specifications

Preventive Maintenance Plans

Regular maintenance is the key to preventing costly breakdowns and extending the life of your HVAC system. Our maintenance plans keep your system running at peak efficiency year-round.

  • Seasonal tune-ups (spring and fall)
  • Comprehensive system inspection
  • Cleaning of coils, blower components, and drains
  • Electrical connection testing and tightening
  • Refrigerant level check and adjustment
  • Thermostat calibration
  • Air filter replacement
  • System performance testing
  • Priority service for plan members
  • Discounts on repairs for plan members

Regular maintenance can reduce energy costs by up to 15% and prevent up to 95% of breakdowns.
