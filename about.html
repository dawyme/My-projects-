About N&D's

Our story, mission, and commitment to excellence in HVAC services

Our Story

Founded in 2005 by brothers Nick and Dave (N&D), our company began with a simple mission: to provide honest, reliable HVAC services to our local community. What started as a small family business has grown into a trusted name in air conditioning and refrigeration services, all while maintaining the values that got us here.

Our journey has been built on hard work, integrity, and a genuine passion for keeping homes and businesses comfortable year-round. We've invested in ongoing training for our technicians, state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment, and a commitment to staying current with the latest HVAC technologies.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional HVAC and refrigeration services that exceed customer expectations while promoting energy efficiency, indoor air quality, and environmental responsibility.

Our Core Values

Integrity

We believe in honest assessments, transparent pricing, and doing the job right the first time.

Expertise

Our technicians are continuously trained and certified on the latest HVAC technologies and techniques.

Reliability

We show up on time, complete jobs efficiently, and stand behind our work with satisfaction guarantees.

Customer Focus

Your comfort and satisfaction are our top priorities. We listen to your needs and provide personalized solutions.
