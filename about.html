About N&D's

10+ years of trusted HVAC, refrigeration and automotive AC excellence in Trinidad & Tobago

Our Story

Founded in 2008, N&D's Air Conditioning and Refrigeration began as a small family business and has grown into one of the most trusted HVAC and refrigeration service providers in Trinidad & Tobago. We are committed to delivering honest, reliable, and high-quality service to every customer.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional HVAC and refrigeration solutions that keep our customers comfortable, businesses running efficiently, and vehicles cool — all with honesty, integrity, and superior craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted air conditioning and refrigeration company in Trinidad & Tobago.

Our Values

  • Integrity and transparency in every job
  • Quality workmanship and reliable parts
  • Customer-first service approach
  • Continuous training and innovation
  • Respect for our community and environment
