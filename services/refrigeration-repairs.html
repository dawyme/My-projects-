Common Refrigeration Problems We Fix

Our technicians specialize in diagnosing and repairing all types of commercial refrigeration systems.

Not Cooling Properly

The most common refrigeration issue - units that run but don't maintain proper temperature.

  • Low refrigerant charge
  • Dirty or blocked condenser coils
  • Evaporator fan motor failure
  • Defrost system malfunction
  • Compressor issues
  • Door gasket leaks

Temperature Fluctuations

Inconsistent temperatures that can compromise food safety and quality.

  • Faulty thermostat or temperature sensor
  • Inadequate airflow
  • Defrost cycle issues
  • Poor door seals
  • Overloading or improper product placement

Water Leaks or Ice Buildup

Excess moisture or ice accumulation indicates drainage or defrost problems.

  • Clogged or frozen drain lines
  • Defrost heater or timer failure
  • Damaged drain pan
  • Excessive humidity infiltration
  • Improper leveling

Unusual Noises

Strange sounds often indicate mechanical or electrical problems.

  • Compressor knocking or clicking
  • Evaporator or condenser fan issues
  • Motor bearing wear
  • Loose or vibrating components
  • Refrigerant flow issues (liquid slugging)

Electrical Problems

Issues with electrical components can prevent proper operation.

  • Tripped breakers or blown fuses
  • Faulty wiring or loose connections
  • Failed start capacitors or relays
  • Defective control boards or timers
  • Motor overload trips

High Energy Consumption

Systems that run constantly or use excessive power often have underlying issues.

  • Dirty coils reducing heat transfer efficiency
  • Worn or failing components causing strain
  • Refrigerant overcharge or undercharge
  • Faulty controls causing excessive cycling
  • Poor insulation or door seal leaks

Preventive Maintenance for Refrigeration Systems

Regular maintenance is especially critical for refrigeration systems to prevent costly product loss and ensure food safety.

Why Refrigeration Maintenance is Essential:

Prevents Product Loss

Regular maintenance helps avoid catastrophic failures that can ruin thousands of dollars in inventory.

Ensures Food Safety

Proper temperature control is critical for preventing bacterial growth and foodborne illness.

Reduces Energy Costs

Well-maintained refrigeration systems operate more efficiently, lowering utility bills.

Extends Equipment Life

Commercial refrigeration represents a significant investment that should be protected through proper care.

Recommended Maintenance Frequency:

Monthly

  • Clean condenser coils
  • Check door gaskets and seals
  • Clean interior and exterior surfaces
  • Verify temperature settings
  • Check for unusual noises or vibrations

Quarterly

  • Inspect electrical connections
  • Check refrigerant levels
  • Test defrost system operation
  • Inspect and clean evaporator coils
  • Check fan motors and blades
  • Verify proper drainage

Bi-Annually

  • Comprehensive system inspection
  • Test safety controls and pressure switches
  • Check and replace worn belts
  • Inspect insulation for damage
  • Lubricate moving parts
  • Check oil levels in compressors

Annually

  • Full system performance evaluation
  • Refrigerant recovery and recycling (if needed)
  • Replace worn seals and gaskets
  • Check and calibrate all controls
  • Verify energy efficiency ratings
  • Update maintenance records and documentation
