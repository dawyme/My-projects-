    Common Walk-In Cooler Problems We Fix

    Understanding common issues helps with early detection and prevention.

    Temperature Not Maintaining

    The most critical issue - failure to maintain set temperature.

    • Insufficient refrigerant charge
    • Compressor failure or inefficiency
    • Condenser coil blockage or failure
    • Evaporator fan motor problems
    • Excessive door opening or poor seals
    • Insulation damage or degradation

    Ice Buildup or Frost Accumulation

    Unwanted ice formation indicates defrost or moisture problems.

    • Defrost timer or heater failure
    • Door left open or faulty seals
    • Evaporator drain line clog
    • Excessive humidity infiltration
    • Improper defrost settings

    Water Leaks

    Water inside or outside the unit indicates drainage problems.

    • Clogged or frozen drain line
    • Damaged or misaligned drain pan
    • Excessive condensation from door openings
    • Plumbing leaks in water-cooled condensers
    • Improper unit leveling

    Unusual Noises

    Strange sounds often indicate mechanical or electrical issues.

    • Compressor knocking, clicking, or hard starts
    • Evaporator or condenser fan motor bearings
    • Loose or vibrating components
    • Refrigerant flow issues (liquid slugging or gas hammer)
    • Electrical arcing or loose connections

    Door Problems

    Issues with doors affect temperature maintenance and energy efficiency.

    • Worn or damaged door gaskets
    • Misaligned or sagging doors
    • Faulty door hinges or closers
    • Stripped or damaged door handles
    • Problems with door sweeps or thresholds
