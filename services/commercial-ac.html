Commercial AC Repair

When your commercial air conditioning system fails, business operations can suffer. We provide rapid response diagnostic and repair services to minimize downtime and get your system back online quickly.

  • Emergency same-day service available
  • Diagnostic service for all commercial AC problems
  • Refrigerant leak detection and repair
  • Compressor and condenser repairs
  • Electrical system troubleshooting and repair
  • Thermostat and control system repair
  • Airflow and ductwork optimization
  • Chiller and cooling tower service
  • Building automation system integration

We service all types of commercial systems including rooftop units, split systems, VRF/VRV systems, chillers, and packaged units.

Commercial AC Installation

Proper design and installation of commercial HVAC systems are critical for energy efficiency, occupant comfort, and system reliability. We provide end-to-end solutions from concept to completion.

  • Comprehensive load calculation and system design
  • Energy modeling and efficiency analysis
  • Ductwork design and fabrication
  • Equipment selection and specification
  • Professional installation following industry standards
  • Building automation and control system integration
  • System testing, balancing, and commissioning
  • Owner training and documentation
  • Post-installation support and maintenance planning

We work with architects, engineers, and building owners to deliver HVAC solutions that meet performance requirements, budget constraints, and sustainability goals.

Commercial Maintenance Programs

Regular maintenance is essential for commercial HVAC systems to maintain efficiency, extend equipment life, and prevent costly downtime that can affect business operations.

  • Scheduled preventive maintenance (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual)
  • Comprehensive system inspection and diagnostics
  • Cleaning of coils, fans, belts, and components
  • Lubrication of moving parts
  • Electrical connection inspection and tightening
  • Refrigerant pressure and level checks
  • Airflow measurement and balancing
  • Condensate drain cleaning and treatment
  • Safety control testing
    • Filter replacement and housing cleaning
  • Performance reporting and efficiency tracking
  • Priority emergency service for contract holders
  • Discounted repair rates for maintenance customers

Our maintenance programs are customized to your equipment type, usage patterns, and facility requirements to ensure optimal performance and reliability.

Commercial Indoor Air Quality Solutions

Indoor air quality in commercial environments affects employee health, productivity, and customer satisfaction. We provide comprehensive IAQ solutions for businesses of all sizes.

  • IAQ assessment and testing
  • Ventilation system evaluation and optimization
  • Air filtration system upgrades (HEPA, MERV 13+)
  • Humidity control and dehumidification systems
  • UV germicidal irradiation for mold and bacteria control
  • Carbon monoxide and pollutant monitoring
  • Energy recovery ventilator installation
  • Demand-controlled ventilation systems
  • Sick building syndrome investigation and remediation

Poor indoor air quality can lead to increased absenteeism, reduced productivity, and health issues. Our IAQ solutions help create healthier, more productive work environments.
