Cleaning & Servicing

  • Detailed service report with findings

Seasonal Maintenance Considerations

Different seasons present different challenges for your HVAC system in Trinidad & Tobago's tropical climate.

Pre-Summer Preparation (March-May)

Prepare your system for the heavy cooling demands of the hot season.

  • Complete system inspection
  • Coil cleaning for optimal heat transfer
  • Refrigerant charge verification
  • Airflow assessment and optimization
  • Condensate system check
  • Thermostat programming for cooling season
  • Electrical system inspection

Rainy Season Considerations (June-August)

Address humidity-related issues common during wet months.

  • Dehumidifier performance check
  • Indoor air quality assessment
  • Drain line treatment to prevent algae growth
  • Outdoor unit corrosion inspection
  • Electrical connection weatherproofing
  • Ventilation assessment for moisture control

Pre-Winter Preparation (September-November)

Prepare heating components for cooler months (where applicable).

  • Heating system inspection (heat pumps)
  • Defrost cycle check (heat pumps)
  • Burner inspection and cleaning (gas systems)
  • Heat exchanger inspection (where applicable)
  • Ventilation and combustion air check (gas systems)
  • Safety control testing

Year-Round Essentials

These items should be addressed regardless of season.

  • Regular filter changes (every 1-3 months)
  • Keep outdoor units clear of debris and vegetation
  • Listen for unusual noises or vibrations
  • Monitor energy bills for unexpected increases
  • Address any performance issues promptly
  • Keep area around indoor units clean and accessible
