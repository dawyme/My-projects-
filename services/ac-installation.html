Our Installation Process

We follow a comprehensive process to ensure your air conditioning system is installed correctly the first time.

1

In-Home Consultation & Assessment

We visit your property to assess your cooling needs, evaluate your space, and take measurements for proper system sizing.

2

System Selection & Recommendations

Based on our assessment, we recommend the best system for your needs, considering efficiency, features, and budget.

3

Site Preparation

We prepare the installation site, ensuring proper electrical connections, mounting surfaces, and clearance requirements.

4

Professional Installation

Our certified technicians install your system following manufacturer specifications and industry best practices.

5

6

7

Follow-Up & Support

We provide post-installation support and schedule your first maintenance visit to ensure optimal performance.

Types of Air Conditioning Systems We Install

We install a wide range of air conditioning systems to suit different applications and preferences.

Residential Systems

Central Air Conditioning

Whole-home cooling systems with indoor and outdoor units connected by refrigerant lines.

  • Ideal for larger homes
  • Consistent temperature throughout
  • Quiet operation
  • Higher initial cost but efficient operation

Ductless Mini-Split Systems

Flexible zoning systems that don't require ductwork, perfect for additions or older homes.

  • Zone-specific temperature control
  • No ductwork required
  • Highly efficient (up to 30 SEER)
  • Easy installation in existing homes

Window Units

Self-contained units installed in windows or walls for single-room cooling.

  • Most affordable option
  • Easy installation and removal
  • Good for apartments or small spaces
  • Can be noisy and less efficient

Portable Air Conditioners

Mobile units that can be moved between rooms as needed.

  • No permanent installation required
  • Good for temporary cooling needs
  • Require venting through a window
  • Less efficient than other options

Commercial Systems

Rooftop Units (RTUs)

Self-contained units installed on building roofs for commercial applications.

  • Space-saving design
  • Easy access for maintenance
  • Good for single-story buildings
  • Available in various sizes and efficiencies

Split Systems

Indoor air handler and outdoor condenser connected by refrigerant lines.

  • Flexible installation options
  • Quiet indoor operation
  • Good for multi-story buildings
  • Can be configured for heating and cooling

Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF/VRV)

Advanced systems that provide precise temperature control to multiple zones.

  • Individual zone control
  • High energy efficiency
  • Simultaneous heating and cooling in different zones
  • Reduced ductwork requirements

Chilled Water Systems

Central plants that produce chilled water distributed to air handlers throughout a building.

  • Ideal for large buildings
  • Highly efficient for large cooling loads
  • Flexible zoning capabilities
  • Long lifespan with proper maintenance
