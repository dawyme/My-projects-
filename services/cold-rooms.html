What is a Cold Room?

A cold room is a specially designed, insulated space that maintains precise temperature and humidity levels for storing temperature-sensitive products.

Temperature Control

Maintain exact temperatures from ambient to deep freezing

Humidity Control

Manage moisture levels for product quality and safety

Customizable Sizes

From small walk-in units to large warehouse-sized facilities

Energy Efficient

Modern insulation and refrigeration systems minimize operating costs

Hygienic Design

Easy-to-clean surfaces meet food safety and pharmaceutical standards

Temperature Zones We Specialize In

Different applications require specific temperature ranges for optimal storage.

Produce Storage (32°F to 55°F / 0°C to 13°C)

Optimal for extending freshness of fruits and vegetables.

  • Leafy greens and herbs
  • Berries and soft fruits
  • Root vegetables
  • Citrus fruits
  • Tropical fruits (ripening control)
  • Mushrooms and fungi

Dairy & Beverages (34°F to 40°F / 1°C to 4°C)

Standard refrigeration for dairy and drink products.

  • Milk, cream, and dairy products
  • Juices and bottled beverages
  • Beer and wine storage
  • Eggs and egg products
  • Prepared salads and dressings

Meat & Seafood (28°F to 32°F / -2°C to 0°C)

Just above freezing to preserve freshness without freezing.

  • Fresh fish and seafood
  • Fresh meat cuts
  • Deli meats and prepared proteins
  • Marinated and seasoned meats
  • Sushi-grade fish

Pharmaceutical (35°F to 46°F / 2°C to 8°C)

Strict range required for most medications and vaccines.

  • Vaccine storage (per CDC/WHA guidelines)
  • Insulin and hormone medications
  • Blood products and plasma
  • Laboratory reagents and samples
  • Diagnostic test kits

Freezer Storage (-10°F to -20°F / -23°C to -29°C)

For long-term frozen storage of various products.

  • Frozen fruits and vegetables
  • Frozen meats and seafood
  • Ice cream and frozen desserts
  • Frozen prepared meals
  • Industrial freezing applications
...

Blast Chilling/Freezing

Rapid temperature reduction for food safety and quality.

  • Hot food chilling (from cooking temp to safe storage)
  • Quick freezing to minimize ice crystal damage
  • Pastry and dough chilling
  • Seafood processing (immediate post-catch)
  • Pharmaceutical rapid cooling

Cold Room Components & Features

We use high-quality components to ensure reliability, efficiency, and longevity.

Structural Components

  • Insulated panels (EPS, XUR, polyurethane)
  • Modular panel systems for easy expansion
  • Heavy-duty flooring options (smooth, anti-slip, drains)
  • Door varieties (swing, sliding, strip curtains, air curtains)
  • Viewing windows and portals
  • Interior and exterior finishes (stainless steel, aluminum, painted steel)

Refrigeration System

  • Condensing units (air-cooled, water-cooled, evaporative)
  • Evaporators (unit coolers, cold plates, coil-in-tank)
  • Compressors (reciprocating, scroll, screw, centrifugal)
  • Controls (electronic, programmable, remote monitoring)
  • Refrigerants (R-134a, R-404a, R-507, R-448a, R-449a, natural refrigerants)
  • Oil management systems
  • Defrost systems (electric, hot gas, ambient)

Monitoring & Controls

  • Temperature and humidity sensors
  • Data logging and recording systems
  • Alarm systems (temperature, door open, power failure)
  • Remote monitoring capabilities (web/app based)
  • Backup power system integration
  • Emergency lighting
  • Access control systems

Interior Features

  • Shelving systems (wire, solid, mobile, pallet rack)
  • Lighting (LED, explosion-proof, motion-activated)
  • Floor drainage systems (floor sinks, trenches, slopes)
  • Air curtains and strip curtains
  • Pallet jacks and material handling equipment
  • Clean-in-place (CIP) systems (for food processing)

Our Cold Room Process

From concept to completion, we ensure your cold room meets all requirements.

1

Needs Assessment & Consultation

We begin with a detailed consultation to understand your specific requirements, including temperature ranges, humidity needs, storage capacity, access patterns, and regulatory requirements.

2

Design & Engineering

Our engineers create detailed designs including layout, refrigeration system sizing, insulation specifications, electrical requirements, and any special features needed for your application.

3

Fabrication & Preparation

We fabricate or source all components, including insulated panels, doors, flooring, and refrigeration equipment, ensuring everything meets quality standards and specifications.

4

Installation & Construction

Our certified technicians handle all aspects of construction, from floor preparation and panel assembly to refrigeration system installation, electrical wiring, and startup procedures.

5

Testing & Commissioning

We thoroughly test the completed cold room, verifying temperature stability, humidity control, door operation, alarm systems, and energy efficiency before handing over to you.

6

Training & Documentation

We provide comprehensive training for your staff on operation, maintenance, and safety procedures, along with complete documentation including warranties, maintenance schedules, and as-built drawings.

7

Ongoing Support

We offer ongoing maintenance programs, emergency service, and technical support to ensure your cold room continues to perform optimally throughout its lifetime.
