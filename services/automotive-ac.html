Common Automotive AC Problems We Fix

Our technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing vehicle AC issues.

AC Not Cooling

The most common complaint - AC runs but doesn't cool properly.

  • Low refrigerant charge
  • Refrigerant leaks
  • Compressor not engaging
  • Clogged expansion valve or orifice tube
  • Faulty condenser fan motor
  • Electrical issues (fuses, relays, wiring)
  • Blend door actuator problems
  • Heater core leaking into AC system

Strange Noises

Unusual sounds when AC is running often indicate mechanical issues.

  • Compressor knocking or clicking
    • ...

Intermittent Cooling

AC works sometimes but not others - frustrating and unreliable.

  • Loose electrical connections
  • Failing relays or sensors
  • Temperature control issues
  • Vacuum leaks (in vacuum-operated systems)
  • Compressor clutch slipping
  • Moisture in the system causing freeze-ups

Bad Odors

Unpleasant smells from AC vents indicate mold or bacteria growth.

  • Mold or mildew on evaporator core
  • Bacteria growth in damp conditions
  • Clogged condensate drain
  • Dirty cabin air filter
  • Chemical leaks from heater core

AC Won't Turn On

No response when AC button is pressed.

  • Blown fuse or tripped circuit breaker
  • Faulty AC switch or control panel
  • Broken wiring to compressor clutch
  • Low pressure switch preventing compressor engagement
  • ECM or PCM not signaling compressor to engage

Excessive Interior Humidity

AC not removing moisture from the air properly.

  • Dirty or clogged evaporator drain

Automotive Refrigerants We Service

We handle all types of automotive refrigerants safely and responsibly.

R-134a

The most common refrigerant in vehicles manufactured 1995-2016.

  • Non-ozone depleting
  • Requires special handling and recovery equipment
  • Working pressure: 25-40 psi (low side), 200-350 psi (high side)
  • We properly recover, recycle, and recharge to specifications

R-1234yf

Newer refrigerant replacing R-134a in vehicles 2017+.

  • Mildly flammable (requires special handling training)
  • Lower global warming potential than R-134a
  • Similar performance characteristics
  • Requires specific equipment for recovery and recycling

R-12 (Freon)

Older refrigerant phased out due to ozone depletion concerns.

  • We can convert R-12 systems to R-134a
  • Proper handling and disposal of recovered R-12

Automotive AC Maintenance Tips

Regular attention to these areas can prevent many common AC problems.

Run AC Regularly

Even in cooler weather, run your AC for 10 minutes weekly to keep seals lubricated.

Replace Cabin Air Filter

A dirty cabin air filter reduces airflow and can cause odors.

Replace every 12,000-15,000 miles or as recommended by manufacturer.

Keep Condenser Clean

The condenser (in front of radiator) needs airflow to work properly.

Regularly clean debris, bugs, and dirt from the condenser fins.

...

Listen for Unusual Noises

Pay attention to any new sounds when the AC is running.

  • Compressor clutch engagement noise
  • Blower motor operation
  • Any squealing, grinding, or rattling sounds

Check for Odors

Musty or moldy smells from vents indicate moisture problems.

Run AC on fresh air mode (not recirculate) occasionally to dry out the system.

Professional Inspection Schedule

Consider annual AC inspections, especially before hot weather seasons.
