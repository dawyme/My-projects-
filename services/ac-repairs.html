Common Air Conditioning Problems We Fix

Our experienced technicians have seen and repaired virtually every AC problem imaginable.

AC Not Cooling

The most common complaint - air conditioning that runs but doesn't cool properly.

  • Low refrigerant levels
  • Dirty or blocked coils
  • Compressor issues
  • Airflow problems
  • Thermostat malfunctions

Strange Noises

Unusual sounds coming from your AC unit often indicate mechanical problems.

  • Grinding or squealing (motor/bearing issues)
  • Banging or clanking (loose parts)
  • Hissing (refrigerant leak)
  • Clicking (electrical issues)

Water Leaks

Water pooling around your indoor unit indicates drainage problems.

  • Clogged condensate drain
  • Damaged drain pan
  • Improper installation
  • Frozen evaporator coil

Electrical Issues

Problems with electrical components can prevent your AC from operating properly.

  • Tripped circuit breakers
  • Faulty wiring
  • Failed capacitors
  • Bad relays or contactors
  • Thermostat wiring issues

Poor Airflow

Weak or uneven airflow reduces comfort and system efficiency.

  • Dirty air filters
  • Blocked or leaking ducts
  • Faulty blower motor
  • Undersized ductwork
  • Closed or blocked vents

Short Cycling

AC turning on and off frequently indicates underlying problems.

  • Oversized system
  • Thermostat issues
  • Refrigerant problems
  • Electrical problems
  • Overheating components

Tips to Prevent AC Problems

Regular maintenance and attention to these areas can help prevent many common AC issues.

Regular Filter Changes

Change or clean your air filters every 1-3 months, depending on usage and filter type.

Keep Outdoor Unit Clear

Maintain at least 2 feet of clearance around your outdoor unit for proper airflow.

Schedule Annual Maintenance

Professional maintenance before each cooling season can identify and prevent problems.

Consider our maintenance plans for regular, hassle-free service.

Watch for Warning Signs

Pay attention to unusual sounds, smells, or performance changes and address them early.

Don't Overwork Your System

Use ceiling fans and close blinds during the hottest parts of the day to reduce cooling load.
