Residential AC Repair

When your home's air conditioning system fails, you need fast, reliable service to restore comfort. Our certified technicians specialize in diagnosing and repairing all types of residential AC systems.

  • Diagnostic service for all AC problems
  • Refrigerant leak detection and repair
  • Compressor and condenser repairs
  • Electrical system troubleshooting
  • Thermostat repair and replacement
  • Airflow and ductwork optimization
  • Drain line cleaning and unclogging
  • Same-day emergency service available

We service all major brands and models, including window units, split systems, portable units, and central air conditioning systems.

Residential AC Installation

Proper installation is crucial for the efficiency, longevity, and performance of your air conditioning system. We ensure every installation meets manufacturer specifications and local building codes.

  • Free in-home consultation and load calculation
  • Proper system sizing for maximum efficiency
  • Professional installation following manufacturer specifications
  • System testing and balancing
  • Customer education on system operation and maintenance
  • Old unit removal and environmentally responsible disposal
  • Manufacturer warranty registration
  • Smart thermostat installation options

We help you choose the right system for your home's size, layout, and cooling needs while considering energy efficiency ratings and long-term operating costs.

Residential Maintenance Plans

Regular maintenance is the most cost-effective way to extend the life of your AC system, maintain energy efficiency, and prevent unexpected breakdowns during the hottest months.

  • Bi-annual tune-ups (spring and fall)
  • Comprehensive system inspection
  • Cleaning of coils, blower components, and drains
  • Electrical connection testing and tightening
  • Refrigerant level check and adjustment
  • Thermostat calibration
  • Air filter replacement with premium filters
  • System performance testing and efficiency reporting
  • Priority service for plan members
  • Discounts on repairs for plan members
  • Extended warranty options

Regular maintenance can reduce energy costs by up to 30% and prevent up to 95% of breakdowns, saving you money and discomfort.

Indoor Air Quality Solutions

Indoor air quality is essential for your family's health and comfort, especially in tightly sealed, air-conditioned homes. We offer solutions to improve the air you breathe indoors.

  • Air purification system installation
  • Humidity control solutions (dehumidifiers/humidifiers)
  • Ventilation improvement assessments
  • Filter upgrades (HEPA, MERV-rated filters)
  • Duct cleaning and sealing services
  • UV germicidal light installation
  • Carbon monoxide and air quality testing

Poor indoor air quality can aggravate allergies, asthma, and other respiratory conditions. Our IAQ solutions help create a healthier home environment.
