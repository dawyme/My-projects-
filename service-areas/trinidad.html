Port of Spain

San Fernando

Chaguanas

One of Trinidad's fastest-growing towns, with new residential developments, plazas and food businesses along the main corridor.

Arima

Couva

Sangre Grande

Rio Claro

A rural east-coast town surrounded by forestry and farming country.

Mayaro

Diego Martin

A western valley of residential communities, apartments and small businesses just outside Port of Spain.

Tunapuna

A central town anchored by the UWI campus and a busy commercial strip.

Arouca

Barataria

San Juan

Marabella

La Brea

Fyzabad

Moruga
